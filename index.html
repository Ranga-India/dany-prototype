Defense Association of New York · Since 1965

New York’s civil defense bar, together.

For more than half a century, DANY has brought together attorneys and qualified non‑attorneys across New York who devote their practice to litigated cases, primarily for the defense.

About DANY

A community built on collegiality and craft.

DANY connects defense practitioners with peers, in‑house counsel and the judiciary — through continuing legal education, a respected journal, and a calendar of gatherings that has run for generations.
